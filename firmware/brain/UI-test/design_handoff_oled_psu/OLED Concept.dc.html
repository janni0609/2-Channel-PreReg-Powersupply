Reading each channel
Large rows are the live measured Voltage and Current. Power sits top-right; the SET strip carries the target V / A limits — always shown, never hidden.
Resolution shown
V 0–35.000 (3 dp) · A 0–2.0000 (4 dp) · P 0–70.00 W. Set values step coarser: 35.00 V / 2.000 A. The sample shows CH1 in voltage-limit and CH2 clamped at its 2.000 A current limit.
